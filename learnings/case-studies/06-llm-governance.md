# Case 06 - Multi-LLM research governance

Observation window: 2026-06-22 through 2026-07-10
Disclosure mode: anonymized
Lifecycle recheck: 2026-08-10
Duration: several weeks of concentrated work followed by a one-month recheck
Application domain: intentionally withheld

## Interpretation warning

This campaign moved from manual design comparison to an increasingly structured autonomous harness.
Model observations must be scoped to their dates, routes, and harness maturity. Internal validation
regions were repeatedly consulted, so later passes were development evidence rather than untouched
promotion evidence. No continuous deployment candidate from this campaign remained approved at the
August 10 lifecycle recheck.

## Exposure

- Usage pattern: one concentrated research campaign with repeated model calls, independent design and audit sessions, and later targeted validation.
- Project phases: design review, evaluator construction, automated discovery, validation, deployment preparation, independent audit, and reassessment.
- Search intensity: very high.
- Parallelism: limited proposal parallelism, with measurement mostly serialized through one evaluator transaction at a time.
- Human involvement: frequent steering and tightly supervised consequential review.
- Resource intensity: heavy relative to the project.
- Breadth: candidate generation, evaluation, research memory, risk review, and research-to-deployment authority.
- Decision influence: critical to research direction and validation discipline, but advisory rather than sufficient for deployment.

## Starting infrastructure

On 2026-06-22, the project had a domain evaluator, historical validation splits, research notes, and
manually created disposable worktrees. Three independent design proposals ran in writable worktrees.
There was no persistent multi-model orchestrator, uniform route ledger, common transactional candidate
boundary, or fully explicit distinction between discovery and promotion authority.

## Ending infrastructure

By 2026-07-10, workers used a uniform CLI and a runner, orchestration loop, transactional evaluator,
and designated judgment layer. The evaluator was read-only and hash-guarded. Workers used disposable
or isolated scratch space. The harness had structured diaries, exclusion maps, pre-result and
post-result reflections, deterministic canned experiments, compile and parse guards, baseline
restoration, deduplication, staged evaluation, time-based validation, and human ownership of expensive
robustness claims.

Claude and Codex could resume exact sessions. Agy retained weaker global continuation semantics.
Timeouts, hard termination, cleanup, and restoration existed, but effective-configuration identity was
incomplete. Measurement was serialized, while progress monitoring relied on logs and manual review.

## Evidence boundaries and lifecycle

The June 22 comparison was design discovery, not promotion. Automated workers later selected on a
training region and repeatedly consulted time-based validation windows. The judgment layer owned
evaluator changes, family closure, robustness batteries, and deployment interpretation. No truly
untouched external promotion source remained after adaptive use of the internal windows.

At the August 10 recheck, later causal and precision corrections had invalidated or weakened the
strongest candidate claims, and newer data weakened the underlying opportunity premise. Automated
operation had stopped and no continuous deployment candidate remained approved. The case therefore
supports methodology and role specialization, not a durable application outcome.

## Project impact

The campaign accelerated exploration, produced useful discoveries, strengthened validation, and built
reusable research infrastructure. Raw proposal volume had mixed value. The largest benefits came from
evaluator improvement, exclusion memory, targeted canned experiments, and independent judgment that
prevented premature promotion.

Without autoresearch, fewer candidate families would likely have been explored and the evidence record
would have been weaker. Without independent judgment and audit, fragile conclusions might have been
promoted sooner. The campaign does not establish that additional automated search alone improved the
final outcome.

## Main findings

### Allocate a scarce strong model to judgment

Verified Fable 5 activity ran from 2026-07-02 through 2026-07-10. The June 30 session previously
included in its window was actually Claude Opus 4.8. Fable generated candidates, but its clearest
marginal value came from classifying apparent results, closing mined families, designing targeted
counterfactuals, auditing evaluator changes, and deciding when to redirect or stop.

Current status: durable as a role-allocation lesson within a mature evidence harness. It is not a
general claim that Fable 5 is the best judgment model.

### Separate candidate reliability from research value

From 2026-06-29 through 2026-07-10, Gemini 3.5 Flash through Agy frequently produced empty,
unparseable, non-compiling, or over-scoped candidate output. One concise post-result abstraction still
introduced a useful mechanism that propagated to other workers.

Current status: qualified. A low-reliability route can be useful for bounded reflection or diversity,
but should remain outside the critical execution path. The result may depend more on the Agy wrapper
and headless contract than on the base model.

### Independent audits need an independent evidence path

On 2026-07-10, GPT-5.6-sol through Codex audited the broader primary record rather than merely scoring
the actor's self-review. It recovered omitted phases, distinguished recommendations from implemented
state, found reproducibility gaps, and reduced deployment optimism. Fable then verified the most
important catches and acknowledged its narrower observation window.

Current status: durable. Audit independence comes from authority and access to primary evidence, not
merely a different model name. Numerical agreement still showed possible anchoring.

### Cross-model design agreement is discovery evidence

On 2026-06-22, Claude Opus 4.8 with Haiku 4.5 subagents led on conceptual framing and validation
discipline, Fugu Ultra supplied the most concrete implementation plan, and GPT-5.5 provided concise
synthesis and lineage checking. Their agreement supported an architectural direction, while their
differences exposed missing assumptions.

Current status: durable as a design-review method, not as promotion evidence or a general model
ranking. Shared repository context can correlate nominally independent proposals.

### Bind model observations to the harness envelope

Across 2026-06-22 through 2026-07-10, several apparent model failures came from ineffective route
flags, stale artifacts, silent configuration, global resume behavior, oversized writable workspaces,
or parsers that rejected useful prose. Apparent scientific wins also disappeared under stronger
causal inputs, precision, validation windows, or robustness batteries.

Current status: durable. Every model claim should bind route, runner, tools, permissions, output
contract, effective configuration, evaluator maturity, and session semantics.

### Separate discovery, validation, promotion, and deployment

From 2026-06-29 through the August 10 recheck, candidates repeatedly changed status after
deduplication, causal corrections, higher-precision evaluation, new seeds, independent audit, or fresh
time windows. No model, loop, or reviewer had unilateral continuous-deployment authority.

Current status: durable. Repeatedly consulted holdouts are internal development evidence. Promotion
requires sealed or newly minted evidence plus an independent owner, and deployment remains a separate
operational authorization.

## Model observations

- Fable 5 through Claude Code, verified from 2026-07-02 through 2026-07-10, was strongest as a judgment owner and designer of high-information experiments. Its self-review retained actor-view blind spots.
- Gemini 3.5 Flash through Agy, observed from 2026-06-29 through 2026-07-10, was unreliable for direct candidates but occasionally useful for concise reflection and diversity.
- GPT-5.6-sol through Codex, observed on 2026-07-10, was effective as an independent full-program auditor. One audit does not support a general ranking.
- Claude Opus 4.8 with Haiku 4.5 subagents, observed on 2026-06-22, was strongest at conceptual architecture and validation discipline.
- Fugu Ultra through Codex on the Sakana provider, observed on 2026-06-22, was strongest at a concrete implementation-oriented plan.
- GPT-5.5 through Codex on OpenAI, observed on 2026-06-22 and later as a worker, was effective at concise synthesis, lineage checking, and higher-volume implementation search.

## Provisional findings

- The evidence does not establish a universally best judgment model.
- Gemini's reflection value rests on a small number of consequential examples.
- Cross-model convergence may indicate either a robust mechanism or correlated search around a mined neighborhood.
- The experimental replacement runner exposed compatibility differences and was not promoted during the window.
- Later bounded application candidates remained operational decisions outside the transferable methodology claim.
