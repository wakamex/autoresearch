# Case 09 - Production-constrained optimization

Observation window: 2026-08-06 through 2026-08-08
Disclosure mode: anonymized
Duration: three calendar days in one concentrated campaign
Application domain: intentionally withheld

## Interpretation warning

This campaign established one narrow sealed promotion through a separately authored clean transaction,
frozen confirmation phases, direct disagreement adjudication, and explicit owner authorization. Other
retained candidates and later batching results remained discovery evidence. A broader promotion
evaluator failed adversarial review and did not become promotion authority.

The current-infrastructure comparison is dated 2026-08-10 and does not extend the original observation
window.

## Exposure

- Usage pattern: one concentrated campaign with repeated experiment, review, and revision cycles.
- Project phases: exploration, implementation, optimization, validation, deployment preparation, and limited post-deployment verification.
- Search intensity: very high.
- Parallelism: primarily sequential measurement with limited parallel proposal, design, and review work.
- Human involvement: frequent steering with a persistent judgment owner.
- Resource intensity: heavy relative to the project, including many tens of transactions, accelerator-hours, multi-gigabyte artifacts, and substantial agent use.
- Breadth: evaluation, orchestration, shared-resource admission, implementation search, promotion, and recovery design.
- Decision influence: critical to several optimization, rejection, and deployment decisions.

## Starting infrastructure

On 2026-08-06, the project had a live resident accelerator worker, an ordinary workload queue, an
existing validation package, and manual operational controls. It lacked a research transaction schema,
frozen research evaluator, structured ledger, exclusion map, disposable candidate workers, persistent
conductor, explicit discovery and promotion routes, complete artifact identity, automatic recovery,
research admission, and progress-freshness monitoring.

## Infrastructure changes

On 2026-08-07, the project added bounded experiment contracts, isolated workers, a production-priority
shared-resource queue, edit boundaries, a campaign conductor, terminal recovery, and activation proofs
covering rejection, neutral, improvement, timeout, and malformed outcomes.

Identity capture expanded to candidate, workload, model artifact, evaluator, lockfile, and effective
configuration. Admission was hardened after live failures involving socket lifetime, released device
state, worker restoration, machine-protocol output, temporary storage, tool availability, and queue
ownership.

Discovery operations were made fail-closed against promotion labels, nonfinite evaluator values were
rejected, and one exact candidate received a separate clean promotion route with frozen broad and
representative confirmation phases.

On 2026-08-08, a broader promotion-evaluator prototype added stronger source, runtime, timing, corpus,
and private-artifact binding. Two adversarial reviews still found authority and integrity escapes, so
the route remained unapproved.

## Ending and current infrastructure

By 2026-08-08, the project had isolated candidate worktrees, structured manifests and results, tracked
and append-only ledgers, bounded process groups, timeout recovery, production-owned admission for
opaque research transactions, resident-runtime restoration, serialized measurement, artifact and
effective-configuration hashes, explicit discovery labels, one narrow sealed-promotion evaluator, and
a designated judgment owner.

The conductor could recover from durable state but was not reliably work-conserving. Progress
freshness and named blocked states were absent. The broader promotion evaluator remained unapproved.

On 2026-08-10, the shared-resource runner and live worker remained active, and runtime dependency
isolation had improved. The managed conductor was inactive with no queued research. The narrow route
remained the strongest promotion authority, while no general sealed-promotion interface existed.

## Project impact

Autoresearch produced useful performance discoveries, strengthened validation, prevented unsupported
changes, and supplied reusable execution infrastructure. It materially changed the deployed
implementation through one narrow promotion. Its largest value came from causal falsification,
adversarial evaluation, and retained research memory rather than continuous autonomy.

Without the workflow, useful changes probably would have taken longer to isolate, and at least one
nonreproducible performance result and one weakly authorized evaluator result might have been mistaken
for promotion evidence. It remains uncertain whether a manually directed sequence of bounded
experiments could have reached the strongest result with less total agent effort.

## Main findings

### Let production own shared-accelerator admission

Designed and activated on 2026-08-07, the queue admitted research as a bounded lowest-priority
transaction through the live worker. One state machine owned admission, lease state, deadlines,
cleanup, worker restoration, and queue recovery. Canned failure paths were exercised before unattended
use.

Current status: active and narrowed. The pattern remained in use on 2026-08-10, but evidence covers one
single-host, low-demand, non-preemptive setting. A running research transaction could still delay a new
live request until its bound expired.

### Separate reasoning parallelism from measurement parallelism

Limited parallel proposal work began on 2026-08-07, with the clearest concurrent design and adversarial
review block on 2026-08-08. Independent designs and critiques exposed issues missed by a linear pass,
while accelerator measurements remained serialized. Parallelism did not increase accelerator
throughput, and some quota-driven tasks duplicated context or ended without a usable artifact.

Current status: active as a qualitative scheduling rule. Quantitative speedup requires a matched serial
comparison. Choose worker count from the backlog of independently specifiable reasoning tasks, not
from unused quota or device availability.

### Scope exact parity to the declared mechanism

On 2026-08-07, a universal exact-parity gate rejected quality-changing candidates that passed
aggregate and protected-case review. Replacing universal parity with an operation-specific quality
contract retained deterministic replay, frozen references, protected limits, and adjudication of every
new disagreement.

Current status: active and narrowed. Exact parity remains appropriate for behavior-preserving claims.
Semantics-affecting changes require a frozen quality contract and recorded adjudication, plus separate
owner authorization for promotion. This does not establish authority over subjective or human claims.

### Evaluator hardening can become a search surface

Evaluator failures appeared throughout 2026-08-07. Two adversarial reviews on 2026-08-08 found that
successive fixes still left caller-asserted admission, unchecked derived numeric behavior, weak time
conservation, runtime impersonation, permissive scalar typing, and artifact replacement races.

Current status: active as a durable warning. The specific broader evaluator is rejected and superseded
as promotion authority. Its proposed smaller runner-owned replacement still requires validation.
Repeated integrity failures should trigger a premise and trusted-computing-base audit, not another
isolated check.

### Process liveness is not campaign liveness

On 2026-08-08, and again during the 2026-08-10 current-state review, execution services and an older
process could be alive while the managed conductor was inactive and no research work was queued.

Current status: an active infrastructure gap requiring implementation and retest. A healthy unattended
campaign needs a recent ledgered result, valid queued or running work, or a named blocked gate. Process
and socket health alone do not establish research continuity.

## Model observation

OpenAI Codex CLI was observed from 2026-08-07 through 2026-08-08 as the persistent judgment-layer
orchestrator, isolated implementation worker, and adversarial evaluator reviewer. Archived worker
records identify the `codex` product route, subscription billing, and high reasoning for the final
rereviews. The campaign model field was absent, so no exact model name can be assigned.

Codex was effective at unfamiliar-code inspection, bounded implementation, deterministic testing,
concrete evaluator attacks, layer-specific diagnosis, and theory revision after counterexamples.
Nearby variants, infrastructure-as-progress, evaluator complexity, and process liveness were recurring
problems. These behaviors depend on shared context, harness maturity, prompting, and the unknown model
route, and there was no controlled within-project model comparison.

The Codex configuration observed on 2026-08-10 is current-harness metadata only. It is not evidence of
the route used during the campaign.

## Provisional findings

- A deterministic filesystem namespace may replace a durable operation registry for bounded single-host recovery, but it was not implemented in the live path.
- Compatible-work batching produced promising discovery throughput but lacks sealed evidence for live admission, waiting, recovery, and delivery behavior.
- Judgment-owner adjudication may suffice for objective output disagreements but cannot replace human-subject or subjective product evidence.
- Parallel proposal and review appeared faster, but no matched serial baseline supports a numerical speedup claim.
